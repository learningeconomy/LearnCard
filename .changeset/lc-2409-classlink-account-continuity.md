ClassLink account continuity (LC-2409), server side. lca-api now enforces who may add a ClassLink personal email (`CLASSLINK_ACCOUNT_CONTINUITY_ENABLED`, off by default; `CLASSLINK_ACCOUNT_CONTINUITY_DEFAULT`; per-district `accountContinuity`) and refuses with a stable reason code. Verifying the personal email makes it the account's sign-in email (its ClassLink Firebase user's email and the contact method, restored exactly on removal or when the school login moves), and returns a custom token to sign back in; the school email keeps signing in through an alias while the district trusts it and the student used ClassLink within `schoolEmailAliasMaxIdleDays` (default 365, per district, 0 turns it off). Email-code login, network hand-off and the Keycloak bridge (which now requires `email_verified`) resolve users through one helper. New routes: `classlink.getAccountContinuity` and `classlink.removePersonalEmail`, with matching `lca-api-plugin` methods. Recovery tokens now record `proof: 'email-otp'`. Escrow records a digest of the sealed contact (remote mode) so continuity can ask a PIN account to confirm its PIN and re-seal to the new email; confirming is limited to 5 an hour, 10 failed per PIN and 2 uncommitted share versions. Security notices also go to the school address while its alias is live, including a new `escrow-reset-started` email template when a PIN reset starts. Deleting an account or its ClassLink data restores the ClassLink login's previous email first. Changing or removing the sign-in email needs a ClassLink or sign-in-email code sign-in in the last 24 hours (reason `recent_sign_in_required`) and sends a new `sign-in-email-changed` notice to the previous address and the school address. `classlink.getAccountContinuity` no longer writes; it reports `reconcilePending`, and the new `classlink.reconcilePersonalEmail` mutation (plugin `classlinkReconcilePersonalEmail`) undoes an interrupted change.
