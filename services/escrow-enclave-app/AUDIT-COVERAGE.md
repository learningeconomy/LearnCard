## Boundaries not overstated

- The monitor authenticates proposals with security-owned key/measurement pins and
  correlates them with authenticated authority-table receipts. It does not decrypt
  envelopes, re-verify hidden PIN verifiers, or possess the owner's private key.
- Commit/Snapshot RPC signatures are verified by clients. They are not persisted
  as such in the authority table; the monitor never relabels a proposal as a signed
  post-commit receipt. Immutable execution rows retain both the proposal and the
  transaction receipt, including reservations whose operation row later changes.
- A coherent privileged purge/rollback of **all** account rows cannot be recognized
  as historical loss by a read-only sweep with no external durable high-water mark.
  An empty fresh generation is legitimate. SCP/resource IAM, generation cutover,
  and independent governance alerts are required; same-generation restore is forbidden.
- Missing receipts/current-row regressions are detected; pending reads, capacity
  limits and a changing scan fence cause retry/coverage alarms, not acceptance of
  partial history. Limits are documented in the monitor runbook.
- Ciphertext durability is not an availability promise. Lost one-shot release
  responses burn a hold; missing auth-share/PIN-salt caches can require recovery
  fallback or explicit repair. The authority never manufactures those values.
- Live IAM/SCP enforcement, real TLS/relay/credential boot, independent alarm
  delivery, reproducible EIF measurements, and the seven-day soak remain mandatory
  operator gates in `STAGING.md`. PR 3 email confirmation/attack C is not covered or
  implemented by PR 2.
