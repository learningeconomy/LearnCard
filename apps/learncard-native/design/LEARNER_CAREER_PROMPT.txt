Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: three-screen native LearnCard career Pathways concept board, continuing the personal Skills and goals board.
Primary request: show a learner-friendly route from personal skills to exploring possible careers and viewing one career. This is career exploration, distinct from the later step-by-step Journeys product.
Style/medium: consistent with LearnCard native: pale neutral lavender paper #EFF0F5, navy #18224E SF-style type, flat white rounded reading panels with NO diffuse panel shadow, small saturated cyan/teal/yellow/pink/violet accents, tiny three-ray marks at the trailing side of headings. Use a few raised satin rendered objects with clean navy outlines and a modest local artwork shadow: a compass and branching path, a design pencil/shape object, a small skills medallion. Vivid but calm, no chrome, floating color blocks or giant decorative gradients.
Composition: one landscape board, THREE matching full portrait iPhone app viewports side by side, no phone hardware. Small outside labels EXPLORE, CAREER RESULTS, CAREER DETAIL. Realistic status bars, navy back controls, generous readable native typography. Every viewport retains the exact existing four-tab white tray: Home with yellow house/pink roof, Passport with blue card, Inbox with pink envelope, Apps with teal rocket; navy outlines/labels and short navy underline under Apps. No fifth tab or desktop sidebar. Exterior footer Design concept · Sample data · AI-enabled example. All occupations/data shown are illustrative, not personalized live recommendations.
