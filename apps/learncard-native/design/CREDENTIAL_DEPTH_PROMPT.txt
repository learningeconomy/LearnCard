Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: visual direction board for an existing native iOS LearnCard app, two close-up credential cards side by side, not complete phones.
Primary request: Make these pink credential cards feel tactile, dimensional and joyful while keeping the established playful bold navy outlined illustration style. Left: a horizontal Passport latest-issued card, stationary. Right: the exact same card in a credential detail screen, gently tilted in perspective with a visible soft cast shadow and a restrained diagonal light reflection. This is an implementation reference achievable with SwiftUI layers, not photorealism.
Scene/backdrop: light cool neutral #EFF0F5 canvas, generous crop margin around each card for its shadow. Category color vivid pink #F9A8D4, dark navy #18224E text.
Card design: rounded corners, thin highlighted top edge, small pink bevel/thickness along the bottom, gentle tonal pink light across surface, broad barely-visible embossed curved contour lines in empty corners. A white/light pink raised circular artwork medallion on the left with inset ring and soft offset shadow. Replace the current large trophy with an original cheerful achievement emblem: a bold yellow five-point shooting star, pink/cyan trailing ribbon marks, tiny celebratory accent strokes, slightly irregular friendly navy outlines. No trophy, no stock emoji. Avoid busy random confetti behind text.
Text, verbatim, on both cards: 'A brilliant milestone' with secondary line 'Hill Valley School' and small category 'Achievements'. Left includes a small circular chevron. Right no chevron. Large clear title, readable secondary text.
Materials: satin paper or lightly embossed collector card. No glossy credit card branding, no holographic rainbow, no heavy white border, no extra fake stacked cards in detail. Preserve calm neutral canvas and strong saturated category accents. Keep flat illustrated emblem beautifully drawn but use shading/rings to give its container depth. No lettering across the illustration. No watermark.
