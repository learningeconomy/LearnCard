Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: a polished four-screen native iPhone concept board for LearnCard personal Skills and goals; a product design reference, not production UI pixels.
Primary request: Design the learner-facing Skills flow that follows the existing LearnCard native Home/Passport/Inbox/Apps experience. Friendly, clear, inviting and useful to a student. Distinguish skills attached to credentials from self-added skills; show provenance without claiming mastery or verification.
Style/medium: refined native iOS interfaces on neutral pale lavender paper #EFF0F5, substantial navy #18224E SF-style typography, generous but practical spacing, flat white rounded reading panels, bright saturated teal/cyan/blue/pink/yellow/violet accents, small hand-drawn three-ray colored marks beside titles. A few raised, beautifully rendered medallion illustrations with navy outlines and a small local shadow give the artwork depth, like the approved dimensional credential card. The ordinary reading panels themselves have NO diffuse drop shadows, shiny surfaces or gradients. This is a continuation of the real app, not a web dashboard, child game or generic AI app.
Composition: landscape board with FOUR equal portrait app viewports side by side, approximately 402x874 logical points each, no phone hardware. Fine dividers and small labels OUTSIDE the viewports: FIRST VISIT, YOUR SKILLS, SKILL SOURCES, PERSONAL GOALS. Show realistic native status bars. Keep the existing fixed four-tab white bottom tray on every screen, with small colored outlined house/passport/envelope/rocket icons and exact labels Home, Passport, Inbox, Apps; Apps selected. No fifth tab, side menu or floating nav island. Deep screens have the established circular back control and centered short navigation title, then a strong content heading with colored ray marks. One coherent type scale and component language throughout. Small exterior footer: Design concept · Sample data.
Screen 1 FIRST VISIT:
Navigation back Apps, centered Skills. Main heading Your skills with three teal rays. A compact original rendered illustration: three dimensional colored skill medallions/cards with a speech-bubble, star and puzzle motif, no trophy. Headline See what you're learning. One sentence Find skills in your credentials or add your own. Navy pill primary button Add a skill. Quiet bordered button Open Passport. A flat white action row beneath: Your goals / Choose what you'd like to work toward. / Add a goal with a clear chevron. Keep this an empty first visit, no invented skill counts, percentages, streaks or mastery score.
Screen 2 YOUR SKILLS:
Navigation back Apps, centered Skills. Heading Your skills with teal rays. Search field Find a skill. Compact filter pills All (selected navy), From credentials, Added by you. Section label 3 skills with a quiet Add a skill action. Three substantial white rows with small saturated icon tiles and chevrons: Communication / From 2 credentials; Teamwork / From 1 credential; Problem solving / Added by you · Proficient. The self-assessed row has a small clear Self-assessed tag, not a verified checkmark. Below, a flat goal panel Your goals / Present with confidence / Edit. At the bottom above the tray, a modest link Explore career pathways with an arrow, no AI promise or match percentage. The source labels must be visually clear and not depend on color alone.
Screen 3 SKILL SOURCES:
Back Skills, centered Skill. Small raised cyan speech-bubble medallion and heading Communication. Body Share ideas clearly and listen to others. Discreet tag From your Passport. Section Where this appears with two flat white source rows: Community leadership / Issued by Community Skills Lab / View credential; Group project / Issued by Riverdale School / View credential. These are links to original signed records, not proof that a learner has mastered a skill. Lower section My assessment / Not added yet / Add my assessment, with a clear self-report label. No blanket verified skill badge, no stars as a performance rating, no mastery gauges, no institution/admin tools.
Screen 4 PERSONAL GOALS:
Navigation title Your goals with a Cancel button. Large heading What would you like to work toward? with yellow ray accents, short enough to wrap naturally in two or three lines. A small navy-outline rendered compass/pencil object beside the short caption, not a giant hero. Two saved editable goal rows Present with confidence and Explore creative careers with tidy remove controls. Persistent label Add a goal above a white native text field; visible character hint 0 / 35; clear Add action. A quiet collapsed row Career details (optional), not a required onboarding step and no salary demand. Small link Review sharing settings. Fixed bottom navy Save goals button above the existing four-tab tray. No auto-saving claim, AI consent checkbox preselection, instant recommendation promise, public/private guarantee, progress percentage or analytics jargon.
Constraints: use the exact short copy where specified; all four screens consistent with an iOS learner app, clear real buttons, minimum-looking 44pt targets, no enormous empty header gap, no gray-on-gray disabled primary CTA, no decorative floating blocks or perpetual motion on reading screens, no logotype duplicated in a detail header, no skill-framework editing, taxonomy management, teacher/admin modes, course/journey authoring, gamified levels, fabricated AI results or salary-gated onboarding. Treat names and credentials as clearly fictional sample content. Make the source distinction, tappable actions and goal-save hierarchy easy to scan.
